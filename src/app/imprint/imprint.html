About this project

Code à Cuisine is a private learning and portfolio project, built during the training at the Developer Akademie. It is not run for profit. The recipes are created by an AI and are meant as inspiration. Please check ingredients, cooking times and allergens yourself before you cook.

Your input and the cookbook

The ingredients and preferences you enter are sent to an automation server and to an AI service to create your recipes. Every created recipe is saved in the public cookbook, where everybody can read it. Please do not enter personal data.
